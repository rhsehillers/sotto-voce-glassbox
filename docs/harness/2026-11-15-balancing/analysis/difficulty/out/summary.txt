DIFFICULTY ESTIMATE - Sotto Voce: The Cut (15.11.2026). Scripts: ../agent.py ../session.py ../run_difficulty.py
All human-behaviour numbers are OWN ASSUMPTIONS (no data on real players). Bands = optimistic / central / pessimistic profile.

PLAYER MODEL
- Agent drives svsim.world.Sim per tick (sim/ untouched). Intended-route skeleton; reactive evasion (steps out of the
  guard's line of sight / backs off when an eye visibly rises, after a reaction time; pushes through after N evasions).
- 14 mistake classes (own clothes in the wings, untimed change, each lever missing, smock on stage, ignores Brac's 4 m,
  corridor unchecked, no shaft escape, running, creeping in sight, wings exploration with curiosity lever pulls, no map
  to box 7). Novice draws each with p 0.35-0.9, practised 0.05-0.10.
- Skill noise: reaction 0.6-1.3 s (practised 0.35-0.6), notices the eye at NoticeLevel+0.10 (-0.05), window reaction
  0.5-3 s, hesitation 0.5-3 s per leg, +-30 % error on the "6 m" change distance; 20 % of novices wait for the interval.
- Learning: the class blamed by the catch (phase + guard + reason) is fixed with p 0.7 (0.9), any other with 0.10
  (0.30) per failed attempt; delays x0.85 per attempt. Real time = 45 s orientation + evenings + 6 s per restart + film.
- Validation: no-mistake agent wins 95/100 (scripted route 200/200; the gap is the post-theft escape past Brac).

VERDICT: a first-time jury member is caught ~3 times (band 2-5) and needs ~7.5 min (band 5-11 min median) for the first
win; ~1 in 3 (band 1 in 7 .. 1 in 2) has not won after 10 min, ~1 in 8 (1 in 20 .. 1 in 4) not after 15 min.
Borderline for a 10-15 min jury; difficulty is knowledge (which lever lures whom, robe for the stage, shaft escape,
where box 7 is), not reflexes. Confidence: low-medium (model of humans unvalidated; comprehension without HUD texts
could push novices to the pessimistic band or beyond).
